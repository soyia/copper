# Names and endorsement

The AGPL grant covers software copyright, not a right to imply that a modified fork, hosting provider or commercial service is officially operated, certified or supported by the Copper project. Preserve truthful attribution and existing notices, identify modifications, and choose branding that avoids confusion. Descriptive references to the upstream project remain possible. No extra noncommercial, no-hosting or no-resale restriction is added to AGPL-covered code.
